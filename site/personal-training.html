Frequently Asked Questions

Do you need a membership to work with us?
For our in person training session at Paradigm Gyms you will be required to have a membership in addition to your training agreement with Vital Athletic. We take care of the membership that allows full access to the gym for those who train 8 or more sessions a month.  For those that train less you will be required to set up a ‘Training Membership’ that will be an additional $89 a month.
What methods of payment do you accept?  
We accept all major payment methods, including credit/ debit, and cash.
What can I expect from a consultation?
Our consultations are a chance for our team members to evaluate how to best serve you. This initial meeting usually last about an hour and may include a movement screening to aid in your individualized program design. After this initial sit-down our team member will connect you to our Functional Nutritionist and/or Metabolic Specialist depending on your goals.
Do you offer meal plans?
Knowing that flavor preferences and foods vary vastly person to person we do not currently offer full meal plans. However sample plans are available to those that work with our Functional Nutritionist.
Do you offer online coaching?
Yes! We offer online and nutrition coaching via our app. We also offer online fitness Coaching via our app.
 Do you do partner or group training?
We do, these sessions are a perfect way to stay motivated with the help of your community.
