Virtual Nutrition Coaching

Online Nutrition Coaching for the individual who struggles with knowing what and how to eat as well as staying accountable for their goals.

Eat Well, Move Better,
​feel energized

Your nutrition habits determine how well your body can perform during your everyday activities as well as in your workouts. Our virtual nutrition coaching service looks at the whole person to create a personalized nutrition plan, holistic and researched back approach. You'll learn how to make smarter food choices, address ongoing health issues through nutrition and create habits. For best results pair it with our metabolic services!
INITIAL ASSESSMENT
We'll discuss your current nutrition habits, assess areas of strength and improvement and determine your nutrition goals.
PERSONALIZED NUTRITION  PROGRAMS
​Based on your goals and lifestyle we'll set specific goals and address nutrition habits that will make the biggest impact.
BI-WEEKLY CALLS
We'll chat every two weeks to discuss progress, challenges, celebrate wins and set new targets for the following weeks.
IN-APP NUTRITION TRACKING
Tracking your food intake will be super easy via our smartphone app, so we can keep track of your habits and tweak when needed.
24/7 MESSAGING SUPPORT
You can use the in-app messaging feature to ask any questions, discuss challenges and clarify ideas so you can stay on track.
OPTIONAL METABOLIC TESTING
This feature might be needed if you feel like you eat all the right kind of foods in the right amount with no results.
